汉语水平考试 · HSK 3

HSK 3 Mock Test

A full computer-based style practice paper: Listening, Reading and Writing, timed and scored like the real exam.

80 questions ~90 minutes Pass mark 180 / 300 Current (2.0) syllabus

Structure

  • 听力 Listening — 40 questions, ~35 min. Each recording plays once.
  • 阅读 Reading — 30 questions, 30 min.
  • 书写 Writing — 10 questions, 15 min (sentence ordering + writing characters).
  • Each section is scored out of 100; total out of 300; 180 to pass.

Exam-mode rules

  • Sections are timed and run in order. When a section's time ends, it moves on automatically.
  • You cannot return to a previous section once you leave it.
  • Listening audio can be played only once per question, as in the real test.
  • You need a Chinese input method for the writing section (typing characters).
  • Your score and a full answer review appear at the end.
